The Core Triad

Our Three Foundational Pillars

The three inseparable dimensions that shape every CLC member's life and vocation worldwide.

Pillar One

Spirituality

Rooted in the Spiritual Exercises of St. Ignatius. Daily Examen, interior freedom, and personal discernment to find God in all realities.

  • Daily Examen Reflection
  • 3-Day & 8-Day Silent Retreats
  • Guided Spiritual Direction
Pillar Two

Community

Small Christian communities (6 to 12 companions) meeting regularly. A confidential harbor where faith and life are shared without judgment.

  • Regular Group Discernment
  • Mutual Fraternal Support
  • Diocesan & National Gatherings
Pillar Three

Mission

Faith expressed in solidarity. We serve at apostolic frontiers: youth mentoring, chaplaincy support, and active care for the vulnerable.

  • Solidarity with the Vulnerable
  • Integral Ecology Initiatives
  • Youth Leadership Formation
Spiritual Tool of Saint Ignatius

The Daily Examen (ዕለታዊ የህሊና ምርመራ)

Take 5 minutes of stillness. Follow these steps to review how God moved in your day.

Step 1 of 5 • ምስጋና

Give Thanks for God’s Gifts

Begin by recalling the small blessings of today: the breath in your lungs, a kind word received, a meal shared, or moments of peace. Let gratitude fill your heart.

Pause • Breathe • Contemplate
